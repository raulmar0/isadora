## Scope
Pays et nationalités (`pays-nationalites/`, served at /pays/): a timed classroom game inside Isadora Gazzi's established world (cream paper, blue ink, gold/ochre/teal details, Fraunces + DM Sans). Mode: Operate (a timed typing task), on student phones, school laptops (1366×650 visible) and classroom projectors.

## Brief (from Isadora, pinned)
Style "name X countries in a minute", countries and nationalities. Centre: the map with the country names to complete. Around: the flags with the nationalities, shown in masculine and feminine. Countries always come with their article. If the student says one form, both appear. User choices: one answer completes both the country and its nationality (24 answers); the article is optional when typing but always displayed, and a wrong article is corrected; 1, 2 or 3 minutes, one by default. The previous hand-drawn SVG map was rejected: a real map is required.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the board is the game — a real atlas plate framed by 24 flag cards that fill in as the student types; no separate home screen, no modal results.
OWN-WORLD: paper ground, sea #d4e5df, land cream, target countries sand, found countries teal with ink label pills (article in gold), missed answers ochre. Fraunces only for the title, score and start heading; DM Sans for everything else.
STORY: the student sees the map and flags, picks a duration, types; every answer pops a name on the map and both nationality forms on the flag; at the end the same board becomes the answer key.
FIRST VIEWPORT: wide screens — bar (back, title, clock, answer field with feedback under it, score, Terminer, sound, rules); six flag cards on top and bottom, six down each side, the Patterson world map in the centre with a Europe zoom in the South Pacific corner. Ready state overlays a start card on the map. Phones — sticky bar with the answer field, world map, Europe zoom at full width, flag cards in two columns showing the country too.
FORM: surface inside the established world; composition pinned by the brief, no concept roll.
FINISH: unreviewed is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review and its verdict.
